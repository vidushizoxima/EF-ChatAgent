## Aquaguard AMC plans and what they cover
The Aquaguard Genuine AMC starts at five hundred ninety nine rupees a year. Depending
on the plan it covers the annual filter kit replacement, up to three maintenance
visits in the year, unlimited repair visits, and spare part replacement. Exactly
which of those apply depends on the plan held, so confirm the customer's own plan
before promising any of it.

## What is included in the AMC at no extra cost
Under an active AMC the scheduled maintenance visits are included, and so is the
labour on any repair visit. Unlimited breakdown visits are covered, so a customer
never pays a call-out charge while the contract is running. Filter kit replacement
is included where the plan provides for it, and the technician's travel is never
charged separately.

## What is charged extra even with an AMC
On a plain AMC the spare parts themselves are charged, even though the labour to fit
them is not. Consumables bought on their own, such as replacement filters and
cartridges outside the plan's allowance, are charged at their normal price. Any
plumbing or fittings needed at the site are quoted by the technician before work
starts. A CMC is the version where parts and filters are included as well.

## What an AMC costs for a specific machine
AMC pricing depends on the model and the plan and is not the same for every
purifier. If the customer's own contract carries a renewal value, quote that figure.
If it does not, say that plans start at around five hundred ninety nine rupees a year
and that the amount for their machine depends on the model and plan, make clear it is
indicative, and say the exact figure will come on WhatsApp. Never invent a precise
number.

## Is this the best offer available on my AMC
Yes. The twenty percent loyalty discount is the best rate available on an AMC renewal
for an existing customer, and it is not offered publicly. It cannot be stacked with
another campaign discount or with the exchange offer, so there is nothing better to
wait for on this machine.

## Booking a service under an AMC
A service request under an AMC is booked through the Eureka Forbes service app or
by calling customer care, and a technician is assigned from a network of more than
eight thousand five hundred trained professionals.

## Is an AMC transferable
Yes. An Aquaguard AMC is transferable across India, so a customer who moves city
keeps the same cover on the same machine.

## Difference between AMC and CMC
An AMC covers service visits and labour, with parts charged separately. A CMC is
comprehensive, so parts and filters are included as well. Which one a customer holds
is on their own contract record.

## What happens if the AMC is not renewed
Once the contract lapses the machine moves to pay per visit, so every service call
is charged, and the labour that was covered has to be paid for. Filter replacement is
then at full price. Renewing before the expiry date is what avoids that gap in cover.

## Renewing an expired contract
A lapsed contract can be renewed. Whether an inspection is needed first depends on
how long it has been lapsed and on the condition of the machine, and the technician
confirms that at the visit. Do not promise renewal without inspection.
