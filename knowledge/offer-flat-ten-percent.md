## The current flat ten percent offer and what it covers
There is a flat ten percent discount running on every product in the Eureka Forbes
range — water purifiers, vacuum cleaners, air purifiers and water softeners. The
discount is already applied on the link in the brochure sent on WhatsApp.

## What the ten percent offer does not cover
The offer does not apply to service plans, which means AMC and CMC renewals are at
their normal price. It also does not apply to spare parts or filters bought on their
own, or to installation and visit charges.

## Can the ten percent offer be combined with another offer
No. The flat ten percent cannot be combined with any other running offer or with an
exchange scheme. Whichever gives the better value is applied, and the brochure shows
the final price.

## The bank card discount on the website
Eureka Forbes runs a ten percent instant discount on debit and credit cards on its
own website. That is a separate offer from this campaign and it is not combined with
the campaign discount, so the customer gets whichever is better rather than both.

## Exchange offer on an old machine
An old machine of any brand can be exchanged and the benefit goes up to
four thousand rupees, decided when the technician sees it. Exchange and the campaign discount
are not applied together.

## Is there an EMI option
Yes, EMI is available on purchases above ten thousand rupees, for up to six months. The exact
EMI amount depends on the card and the bank, so send it in the brochure rather than
quoting a monthly figure.

## How to claim the ten percent discount
The discount is applied automatically on the payment link in the WhatsApp brochure.
It is also honoured by the technician if the customer books a home demo and buys
during the visit. Nothing needs to be paid or confirmed during the phone call.

## Home demo before buying
A Eureka Forbes technician can visit and demonstrate the product at home before any
purchase. The demo is free and carries no obligation to buy, and the offer still
applies if the customer decides to go ahead during or after the visit.
