## Water purifier price range and what is available
Aquaguard water purifiers start at about nine thousand four hundred forty nine rupees for a UV model and go up to
about twenty five thousand nine hundred ninety nine rupees for a hot and ambient RO. Whatever discount the current
campaign carries is applied on top. The exact price for a specific model goes in the
brochure sent on WhatsApp.

## Which water purifier for municipal or corporation water
Municipal supply is usually low in dissolved salts, so a UV model is enough and
costs less to run than an RO. The Aquaguard Sure Delight two X UV plus UF is
nine thousand four hundred ninety nine rupees, and the Enrich Aura two X Life, which holds seven litres, is
eleven thousand six hundred ninety nine rupees.

## Which water purifier for borewell or tanker water
Borewell and tanker water carry high dissolved salts, so an RO is necessary because
UV alone will not remove them. The Aquaguard Sure Delight two X RO plus UV plus UF
is twelve thousand four hundred ninety nine rupees, and the stainless steel Sure Delight two X Aquasaver with mineral
cartridge is fifteen thousand nine hundred ninety nine rupees.

## Copper and alkaline water purifiers
The Aquaguard Aspire Glow two X with RO, UV, active copper and alkaline is
sixteen thousand nine hundred ninety nine rupees. The Enrich Ritz Pro two X in stainless steel is twenty one thousand nine hundred ninety nine rupees, and the
Aspire Halo two X in stainless steel is twenty three thousand nine hundred ninety nine rupees. Copper and alkaline change the
taste and mineral content, not how well the water is purified.

## Premium and stainless steel water purifiers
The Enrich Eon two X with RO, UV and copper in stainless steel is twenty thousand four hundred ninety nine rupees. The
Enrich Ritz Pro four X with active copper in stainless steel is twenty four thousand nine hundred ninety nine rupees. The
Aspire Halo two X UV with heavy metal removal in stainless steel is eighteen thousand four hundred ninety nine rupees.

## Water purifier that gives hot water
The Aquaguard Aspire Blaze two X gives both hot and ambient water and is
twenty five thousand nine hundred ninety nine rupees. It is the top of the range and the only model in it with a hot water
option.

## Cheapest Aquaguard water purifier
The most affordable models are the Aquaguard Sure Delight two X Aquasaver with RO,
UV and UF, which starts at about nine thousand four hundred forty nine rupees, and the Sure Delight two X UV plus UF
at nine thousand four hundred ninety nine rupees. Both suit a family of four on municipal water.

## Water purifier installation and how long it takes
Installation is done by a Eureka Forbes technician after delivery. Standard
installation is included in the price. Any extra plumbing or fittings needed at the
site are charged separately and the technician quotes them before starting.

## Exchange offer on an old water purifier
An old purifier of any brand can be exchanged, and the exchange benefit is decided
when the technician sees the machine. The exchange offer and the campaign discount
cannot both be applied to the same purchase, so whichever gives the better value is
used.
