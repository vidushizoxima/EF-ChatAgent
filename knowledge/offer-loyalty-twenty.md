## The loyalty programme and what the twenty percent covers
Existing Eureka Forbes customers get twenty percent off any AMC renewal, and
twenty percent off any new product purchase. It is not a public discount — it is
for customers who are already with us. The renewal half applies to the plan on the
machine they already own, and the product half applies across water purifiers,
vacuum cleaners, air purifiers and water softeners.

## Which product categories the twenty percent applies to
Four categories qualify: water purifiers, vacuum cleaners, air purifiers and water
softeners. If a customer asks what is available, ask which of these they are
interested in before naming anything, because reading out four ranges on a call is
useless to them.

## What the loyalty twenty percent does not cover
Consumables bought on their own are excluded, so replacement filters and cartridges
are at their normal price. It cannot be combined with the exchange offer or with
another campaign discount — whichever gives the better value is applied. Spare parts
charged during a service visit are also outside it.
